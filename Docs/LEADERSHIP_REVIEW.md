# Leadership Review

Canonical **execution-quality** analysis of meetings (and, later, other operating rituals). Meetings: [MEETING_INTELLIGENCE.md](MEETING_INTELLIGENCE.md).

## CURRENT

Not implemented as a product.

Related: group knowledge extraction, synthesis narratives, proactive heuristics. None of these is Leadership Review.

---

## TARGET

After a meeting is analyzed, LAVR may score **observable execution quality**, for example:

- how many tasks were formulated;
- how many have an owner;
- how many have a deadline;
- which agreements stayed vague;
- which decisions were discussed but not confirmed.

Communication **patterns** may be noted only as observables (“three action items had no owner”).

**Do not** do pseudo-psychological diagnosis of the CEO or staff.

Boundaries:

- facts from the transcript and structured extraction only;
- no personality labels, no “morale”, no clinical language;
- output is operational: gaps in ownership, deadlines, decision clarity.

Phase 9. Depends on Meetings (Phase 5A; Zoom-sourced meetings from Phase 5B) and preferably Commitments (Phase 6).
